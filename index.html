Why Neuron

Recovery starts with how your nerves and muscles work together

After a stroke, a nerve injury, surgery or a long-standing pain, the body has to relearn movement. We assess how you move, then plan therapy step by step, with guided sessions and equipment for gait, balance and strength.

Your care plan

How your treatment works

  1. Assessment

    The doctor listens to your history and checks how you move.

  2. Diagnosis

    You hear what is causing the problem, in plain words.

  3. Treatment plan

    You get a plan for therapies and sessions that fits your case.

  4. Therapy

    Guided, hands-on sessions with gait, balance and electrotherapy equipment.

  5. Recovery

    We track your progress and adjust the plan as you improve.

  6. Follow-up

    Check-ins to keep your gains and prevent the problem returning.

Can't travel? We come to you.

Our home service covers the full cycle: assessment, therapy and follow-up, in your own space. It suits patients who find it hard to travel, such as after a stroke or surgery.

Do I need an appointment?

An appointment keeps your wait short. Send the form above and we will confirm a time by phone.

Yes. Neuro rehabilitation is a core service, led by a physiotherapist with a fellowship in neuro rehabilitation.

Can you treat my child or an elderly family member?

Yes. We have a Child Development Center, pediatric rehab and geriatric rehab.

Do you offer home visits?

Yes. Home service includes assessment, therapy and follow-up. Message us on WhatsApp with your area and condition.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the cause and how long you have had the problem. The doctor tells you after the first assessment.
